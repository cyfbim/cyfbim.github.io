Formwork Master

Add-in for Autodesk® Revit® 2025, 2026 and 2027

Formwork takeoff for columns, beams, walls, floors, foundations and stairs. Each face is measured from the actual geometry, with contact deducted and a formula you can check by hand.

Coming soon to the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace.

Formwork Master will be available there with a free trial. Trials, subscriptions and billing are handled by Autodesk through your Autodesk account.

What it does

  • Creates a thin formwork element on every face of the selected structural elements that needs formwork, with contact from neighbouring elements and topography deducted.
  • Writes each face's level, element type, grid, orientation, formula and area to parameters, and lists them in a schedule.
  • Writes a formula for each face, such as 260 * 180 - (80 * 120), and checks that it adds up to the measured area.
  • Never modifies your structural elements.

Requirements

  • Autodesk Revit 2025, 2026 or 2027 on 64-bit Windows.
  • Signed in to Revit with the Autodesk account that holds the subscription or trial.
  • An internet connection the first time the add-in is used in each Revit session. It keeps working offline for up to 7 days after a successful check.

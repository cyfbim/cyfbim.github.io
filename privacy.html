2. What our add-ins do on your computer

Our add-ins run on your computer, inside Autodesk® Revit®. None of them sends any information to us, and none of them includes analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising. What each add-in reads, writes or sends is listed below.

Auto Join Pro

Auto Join Pro collects nothing. It does not:

  • collect, transmit or store any personal information;
  • read or write any file outside the Revit model you are working on;
  • write settings to your file system or to the Windows registry;
  • connect to the internet or contact any server.

There is no account to create, no setting to turn off, and no data to opt out of. Everything the add-in does happens in memory and ends when you close the window.

Formwork Master

Subscription check. Formwork Master is a paid app. To check your subscription, it sends two values to the Autodesk App Store entitlement service over HTTPS: your Autodesk user ID, as provided by Revit, and the app's ID. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent to us. As with any internet connection, Autodesk's servers also see your computer's IP address. How Autodesk handles this is governed by Autodesk's own privacy statement.

License cache. So that you can keep working offline for up to 7 days, Formwork Master keeps a small file, license.dat, in %AppData%\CYF BIM Studio\Formwork Master\. It contains a hashed form of your Autodesk user ID, a hashed form of your computer's Windows identifier (read from the registry, never changed), the time of the last successful check, and a signature. It never leaves your computer. It is replaced at each successful check, and the add-in deletes it itself if Autodesk reports that you no longer have an active subscription. You can delete it at any time; the add-in will simply check online again.

Reports. If some faces cannot be calculated, or an element fails, Formwork Master writes a plain-text report next to your model file (or in your Windows temporary folder if the model has not been saved). It lists element IDs, the add-in and Revit versions, and the model's file path. The report stays on your computer and is not sent anywhere. It is there for you to review, or to email to us if you want help. You can delete it whenever you like.

Temporary file. While calculating, Formwork Master creates a temporary parameter definition file in your Windows temporary folder and deletes it when the calculation ends.

Formwork Master does not write settings to the Windows registry, and it does not read or write any other file outside the Revit model.

3. Information we receive from the Autodesk App Store

When you download one of our apps, the Autodesk App Store may pass us information about that download, which can include your name, email address and company name.

We use it only to understand how many people use our apps, and to contact you if a serious problem with an app you downloaded comes to light. We do not sell it, rent it, share it with anyone, or add you to a marketing list.

We do not keep our own copy of these records. They remain in the Autodesk App Store publisher portal for as long as Autodesk retains them, and if you want those records changed or removed, please contact Autodesk directly.

How the Autodesk App Store itself handles your data is governed by Autodesk's own privacy statement, not by this policy.

4. Support correspondence

Please do not send project files containing confidential or client information. A cut-down sample file that reproduces the problem is usually more useful anyway.
